## What we collect and why

### When you browse the site

- **Technical request data**: your IP address, browser type and the page you asked for. Our hosting provider processes this to deliver the site and protect it from abuse, and keeps it in short-lived server logs. *Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running a secure website (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).*
- **Language preference**: if you pick a language, we remember it in a cookie (see *Cookies* below).

We don't use analytics, advertising pixels or social-media trackers. The Instagram and TikTok buttons are plain links. Those platforms only receive data if you click through to them, and their own privacy policies then apply.

### When you contact us

The contact and waitlist form asks for your **name, email address, the topic you're interested in and your message**. We use them only to reply to you and, if you ask, to prepare an offer. *Legal basis: steps taken at your request before a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)), and our legitimate interest in answering enquiries (Art. 6(1)(f)).*

### When you have an account

To log in to an AppAmora dashboard we store your **name, email address and a securely hashed password** (we never store the password itself). If you use **Sign in with Google**, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with us, along with sign-in tokens. For each login session we store your **IP address and browser type** so we can keep your account secure. To block password-guessing, we also briefly count login attempts per IP address. *Legal basis: providing the service you signed up for (Art. 6(1)(b)) and keeping it secure (Art. 6(1)(f)).*

The login page can use **Google reCAPTCHA** to tell people from bots. reCAPTCHA collects device and usage data and sends it to Google, under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Terms](https://policies.google.com/terms).

### When we work together

If you buy a plan or a project from us, we keep the contract, invoices and project communication, as tax and accounting law requires. *Legal basis: the contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) and our legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c)).*

Some of these companies are based in the USA, so data may be accessible from there. Such transfers are protected by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

We never sell personal data. We only share it with authorities when the law requires us to.

## How long we keep it

- **Contact messages**: up to 24 months after our last exchange, unless they lead to a contract.
- **Accounts**: while your account is open. We delete it within 30 days after you ask us to close it.
- **Login sessions**: they expire after 7 days. Login-attempt counters are only used to block abuse.
- **Contracts and invoices**: as long as tax and accounting law requires.
- **Server logs at our host**: a short period, according to the host's own retention rules.

## Cookies

We only use cookies the site needs to work, so we don't show a cookie banner.

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| `NEXT_LOCALE` | Remembers the language you chose | 1 year |
| `better-auth.session_token` (may start with `__Secure-`) | Keeps you logged in, only after you log in | 7 days |
| reCAPTCHA cookies (e.g. `_GRECAPTCHA`) | Set by Google on the login page to detect bots | Set by Google |

## Websites built with AppAmora

When the AppAmora website builder launches, each business that builds a site with it decides what happens to the data its own visitors and subscribers submit. For that data the business is the controller and AppAmora acts as its processor. Visitors of those sites should contact the business directly.

## Your rights

Under Albania's law on personal data protection and, for visitors in the EU, the GDPR, you can ask us to **access, correct, delete or export** your personal data. You can also ask us to **restrict** its use, or **object** to our use of it. Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time. Write to [{email}](mailto:{email}). We'll reply within one month.

If you think we've handled your data wrongly, you can also complain to Albania's Information and Data Protection Commissioner (IDP), or to the data protection authority in the country where you live.

## Children

Our services are meant for businesses and adults. We don't knowingly collect data from children under 16.

## Changes to this policy

When our services change, for example when payments or the website builder launch, we will update this page and the date at the top. Important changes will also be announced on the site or by email to account holders.
